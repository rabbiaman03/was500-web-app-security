Part A: Defeat Your Own Validation

Minimal test page with a single required email field enforcing required minlength="5".

DevTools Test Steps:
  1. Try clicking Submit with an empty field or typing "abc" to see browser blocking submission (Screenshot 1).
  2. Right-click the input → Inspect → delete required and minlength="5" from the DOM.
  3. Click Submit again with an empty field to see browser submit without complaint (Screenshot 2).
